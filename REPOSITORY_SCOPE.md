
# Repository scope

## Analysis-level package

The compact derived tables and self-authored scripts reproduce the statistical aggregation and final core evidence. They do not reconstruct every model checkpoint from raw forcings.

## Claim-specific model replay

The one-basin, one-checkpoint replay verifies the executable storage-transition claim over a complete held-out test period. Complete three-seed and multi-basin results are reproduced from the frozen derived tables in the analysis-level package.

Together these two layers cover the manuscript's numerical aggregation and a clean end-to-end model execution.
